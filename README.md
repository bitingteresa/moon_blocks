# 🌙 MOON BLOCKS

A colorful divination app inspired by the Taiwanese temple ritual of **跋桮** (pua̍h-pue in Taiwanese Hokkien, or jiaobei/筊杯 in Mandarin) involving crescent-shaped wooden blocks to receive guidance from the divine — combined with the numbered fortune sticks known as **qiuqian** (求籤). Check it out [here](https://bitingteresa.github.io/moon_blocks/)

## What it does

Cast virtual moon blocks three times to receive a yes, no, or "laughing" (unsure) response for a question you want advice on. The virtual god will then decide if you received enough yes casts to give you guidance on your query. A fortune stick is then drawn that corresponds to a poem with traditional interpretation and a modern western one, called reflection. Passages are drawn from an out-of-print *Chinese Fortune Sticks* by Zhao Xiaomin & Martin Palmer (archived at [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/isbn_9781435109780)).

## About the ritual

In Taiwanese folk religion, moon blocks (跋桮, pua̍h-pue) are a form of cleromancy used to communicate with deities. A pair of crescent-shaped blocks - flat on one side, round on the other - are tossed onto the temple floor one to three times depending on the temple. The combination of faces determines the deity's response:

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| One flat, one round | Yes |
| Both round | No |
| Both flat | The deity is laughing or unsure about your question |

After receiving a positive response, worshippers draw a numbered fortune stick from a tube and retrieve the corresponding numbered poem.
